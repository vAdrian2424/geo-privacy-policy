
# GEO – Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 11 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GEO (Discord Bot ID: 1008369229671698573) collects, processes, stores, and deletes information when you use the bot.

## 1. Data Usage

GEO collects and stores limited information to provide its geography and history games, user profiles, XP system, and global leaderboards. Information is used exclusively for the bot's functionality.

## 2. Data Stored

GEO stores certain information in a database, including:

- Discord User ID
- Experience Points (XP)
- Configured nationality
- Favorite country
- Least favorite country

Answers submitted during games are processed to determine whether they are correct. XP is awarded and saved to your profile when you answer correctly.

## 3. Global Profiles

GEO uses a global profile system. Your profile and XP are shared across all Discord servers where GEO is available. Removing GEO from a server does not automatically delete your profile or XP.

## 4. Data Updates

Users can configure their nationality, favorite country, and least favorite country using GEO's configuration commands. Stored preferences are used for their corresponding features.

## 5. Temporary Caching

GEO may temporarily process or cache active game sessions, submitted answers, timers, and game progress. Temporary information is discarded when it is no longer required.

## 6. Third-Party Services

GEO operates through Discord and uses Discord's API to provide its functionality.

GEO does not sell user information or intentionally share stored profile information for advertising purposes.

[Discord Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy)

## 7. Data Deletion

Users can request deletion of their stored profile information by contacting the developer through the official support server.

Deleting your profile will remove associated information, including XP and configured preferences.

Removing GEO from a server does not automatically delete global profile data.

[Join GEO Support Server](https://discord.gg/a3Ehddddan)

## 8. Changes to This Policy

GEO reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Users are encouraged to review it periodically.
